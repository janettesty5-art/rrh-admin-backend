IKECHUKWU B. ANYANWU

CEO & Founder — Road Rock Holdings

Tech Engineer Ikechukwu B. Anyanwu is a visionary entrepreneur, technology engineer, and infrastructure strategist with a passion for innovation, engineering excellence, and transformational development.

With years of experience spanning technology, engineering systems, construction planning, renewable energy integration, and business leadership, he founded Road Rock Holdings with a vision to create a diversified conglomerate capable of solving modern infrastructure and technological challenges across emerging economies.

"Our goal is to build a future where infrastructure, energy, technology, and smart living solutions work together to improve lives, empower businesses, and shape modern societies."
